## Marketplaces

General freelance platforms. Fees, availability, and rules differ by country and change over time; read each platform's current terms.

- [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/) - Large general marketplace for hourly and fixed-price contracts.
- [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/) - Gig-based marketplace where sellers list fixed-scope services.
- [Freelancer.com](https://www.freelancer.com/) - Bidding-based marketplace with contests and projects.
- [PeoplePerHour](https://www.peopleperhour.com/) - UK-origin marketplace for hourly and fixed-price work.
- [Guru](https://www.guru.com/) - Marketplace with project and work-room features.
- [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/) - Vetted network for experienced developers, designers, and finance experts; selective screening.
- [99designs](https://www.99designs.com/) - Design-focused marketplace with contests and direct projects.
- [Freelancermap](https://www.freelancermap.com/) - Europe-focused freelance project board.
- [Contra](https://contra.com/) - Portfolio-first platform for independent professionals.
- [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/) - Startup jobs and contract roles.
- [We Work Remotely](https://weworkremotely.com/) - Remote job board.
- [Remote OK](https://remoteok.com/) - Remote job board.
- [BDJobs](https://www.bdjobs.com/) - Bangladesh's job portal, useful for local contract and remote listings.

## Getting paid

Neutral notes only. Fees, limits, supported countries, and rules change; confirm on the provider's site and with your bank. Several options may be combined.

- [Payoneer](https://www.payoneer.com/) - Payment platform that issues receiving accounts for marketplace payouts and supports withdrawal to a local bank account. See its [freelancer page](https://www.payoneer.com/solutions/freelancers/).
- [Wise](https://www.wise.com/) - Money transfer service with multi-currency features; availability of account features for Bangladesh residents varies, so check current coverage.
- [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/) - Widely used internationally. Check current service availability for Bangladesh accounts before relying on it.
- [Bank wire (SWIFT)](https://www.bb.org.bd/) - Traditional international transfer to a Bangladeshi bank account. Typically slower, with intermediary and bank charges. Banks handle inward remittance documentation; ask your bank what they require. Bangladesh Bank is the regulator.
- Direct marketplace withdrawal - Many marketplaces offer withdrawal by bank transfer or partner services. Check the "withdraw" section of your marketplace account for the options offered to your country.
- [Dutch-Bangla Bank](https://www.dutchbanglabank.com/), [BRAC Bank](https://www.bracbank.com/), [City Bank](https://www.city-bank.com/) - Examples of Bangladeshi banks that provide foreign-currency and remittance services. Listing is informational, not a recommendation; contact your bank for freelancer-specific accounts and documents.
- Invoicing tools: [Wave](https://www.waveapps.com/), [FreshBooks](https://www.freshbooks.com/), [Invoice Generator](https://invoice-generator.com/) - For direct clients, to produce professional invoices.

## Tax and legal basics

> **Disclaimer:** This section is **not legal, tax, or financial advice**. Rules on income tax, foreign-currency receipts, remittance, and business registration change and may depend on your situation. Read the official sources below and consult a qualified tax professional or your bank. Some official sites block automated checks or are slow from abroad; if one does not load, try again later.

- [National Board of Revenue (NBR)](https://nbr.gov.bd/) - Official tax authority of Bangladesh: laws, forms, and notices.
- [NBR e-TIN registration](https://www.etaxnbr.gov.bd/) - Official portal for Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and online tax services.
- [Bangladesh Bank](https://www.bb.org.bd/) - Central bank and foreign-exchange regulator; see its [circulars](https://www.bb.org.bd/en/index.php/mediaroom/circular) for remittance and forex rules.
- [Bangladesh Telecommunication Regulatory Commission (BTRC)](https://btrc.gov.bd/) - Telecom and internet regulator.
- [National Portal of Bangladesh](https://bangladesh.gov.bd/) - Gateway to government services.
- [ICT Division](https://ictd.gov.bd/) - Ministry-level ICT body; reference for national digital and freelancer-related initiatives.
- [Bangladesh Computer Council (BCC)](https://bcc.gov.bd/) - Government ICT agency; runs training programmes.
- [IRS Form W-8BEN](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-w-8-ben) - US form that non-US individuals may be asked to submit to US-based platforms or clients to certify foreign status.

## Tips

- **Pick a niche.** Specific skills with specific outcomes are easier to sell than "I do everything".
- **Build proof.** A small portfolio, a GitHub profile, or case studies beat long skill lists.
- **Write specific proposals.** Read the brief, reference details, propose a first step. Avoid copy-paste templates.
- **Start with small jobs** to build reviews, but price yourself sustainably.
- **Communicate clearly and on time.** Confirm scope, deadlines, and revisions in writing.
- **Stay within platform rules.** Do not use multiple or purchased accounts, fake reviews, or off-platform payments where prohibited; accounts can be suspended.
- **Never share passwords, OTPs, or PINs** with anyone, including "clients" or "account managers". Be wary of fake jobs that ask for upfront fees.
- **Protect your payments.** Use escrow or milestones with new clients, and keep invoices and records.
- **Keep records for tax.** Save contracts, invoices, and bank statements.
- **Keep learning.** Pair a paid skill with an adjacent one (for example design with front-end, or writing with SEO).
- **Take care of yourself.** Set working hours and keep a reserve for slow months.
